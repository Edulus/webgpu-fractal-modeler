Shape-parameter extremes

Renders every parameterised shape at the minimum, default and maximum of each of its parameters, and checks that a frame actually came out. This is the regression fixture for the failure that mathematics alone cannot catch: a value inside its measured range that nonetheless renders as nothing, because the estimator has stopped converging on real hardware rather than in a Node port.

A pass means the frame is non-empty and no error was raised — not that it is correct. Correctness of the range is what tools/shape-params.test.js and the Node sweeps establish; this guards the step between a measured range and a picture.
