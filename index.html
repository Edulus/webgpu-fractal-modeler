Twenty objects from mathematics

Complex Geometry Explorer

Shapes that come out of equations, symmetry groups and endless repetition — minimal surfaces, stellated solids, tessellations of curved space. Spin them, zoom in as far as you like, and fly inside the ones with an interior. Everything is drawn live, pixel by pixel, as you move.

One renderer, multiple applications

Use the opaque explorer preset for a full-screen navigable artwork, an educational model viewer, a music visual, or an installation display. Orbit and zoom remain responsive while the adaptive quality system keeps the renderer practical across a range of GPUs.

Transparent mode places the same visuals behind ordinary page content. The canvas stays click-through, and the dark composite treatment preserves legibility through controlled bloom, ACES tonemapping, vignette, and dithering. Switch the model, palette, and quality tier using the panel.
